Research & Scientific Computing

My research connects theoretical physics, numerical modeling, and computational methods to investigate quantum materials, electronic transport, electromagnetic phenomena, and applied radiation physics. I develop and use scientific software to study complex physical systems and analyze quantitative data.

Research Areas

01 · Electromagnetic Modeling

Computational Electromagnetics & THz Near-Field Optics

Research involving terahertz near-field electromagnetic response, nanoscale optical phenomena, and computational modeling, including work at Ames National Laboratory.

  • THz near-field response and electromagnetic field modeling
  • Numerical analysis of field distributions and material response
  • Research contributions to nanoscale THz studies of materials
View related project
02 · Condensed Matter Theory

Quantum Transport & Topological Materials

Theoretical and computational studies of electronic transport in low-dimensional and topological quantum systems, including graphene and Weyl semimetals.

  • Berry curvature and Lorentz-force effects in magnetotransport
  • Hofstadter physics in twisted graphene structures
  • Spin-orbit coupling, Hall effects, and electron transport
Explore quantum transport code
03 · Scientific Software

Numerical Modeling & Scientific Computing

Scientific computing for physics-based simulation, data analysis, and reproducible computational research.

  • Python, MATLAB, and Mathematica
  • Fourier analysis, numerical integration, and matrix-based calculations
  • Parameter sweeps, visualization, and simulation automation
  • Parallel and computationally intensive numerical workflows
04 · Applied Radiation Physics

Computational Medical Physics

Computational projects and training that connect quantitative physics methods with radiation dose calculation and medical physics applications.

  • Monte Carlo radiation dose simulation in a water phantom
  • Numerical approaches to dose analysis
  • Interest in computational methods for therapeutic medical physics
