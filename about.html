About Me

I am a Diagnostic Medical Physics Resident at the University of Chicago Medicine, with a background in physics, medical imaging, and quantitative image analysis.

My professional interests include diagnostic imaging physics, image quality, radiation dosimetry, quality assurance, and the application of computational methods to medical imaging. My clinical training encompasses a broad range of imaging modalities, including radiography, fluoroscopy, CT, MRI, mammography, ultrasound, and nuclear medicine.

My research interests focus on quantitative image-quality assessment, observer performance, and developing practical methods for evaluating medical imaging systems.
