About Me

Computational physicist with a foundation in theoretical physics, extensive research experience, and specialized medical physics training.

Professional Background

I hold a PhD in Physics from the University of Florida. My research spans theoretical and computational condensed matter physics, quantum transport, graphene and topological materials, and electromagnetic phenomena.

Through postdoctoral research, including work at Ames National Laboratory, I have applied mathematical models and numerical simulations to investigate complex physical systems. My work includes THz near-field electromagnetic modeling and scientific data analysis, with peer-reviewed publications in Physical Review B, Optics Express, Journal of Physics: Condensed Matter, and Instruments.

I use Python, MATLAB, and Mathematica for scientific computing, numerical analysis, simulation, and visualization. I have also developed practical software tools for technical and research workflows.

My medical physics background includes a medical physics certificate and clinical training involving radiation dosimetry, quality control, equipment performance assessment, and introductory radiation therapy planning activities, including organ-at-risk contouring in Eclipse and IMRT concepts. I am interested in further developing my career in therapeutic medical physics.
