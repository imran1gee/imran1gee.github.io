Clinical Experience

My clinical training in diagnostic medical physics at the University of Chicago Medicine includes quality control, performance evaluation, and clinical applications across a broad range of diagnostic imaging modalities.

Radiography & Fluoroscopy

Performed quality control and annual performance evaluations of diagnostic radiography, portable radiography, fluoroscopy, and interventional imaging systems, including assessment of image quality, radiation output, automatic exposure control, dose metrics, and system performance.

Also gained experience with radiation shielding design and peak skin dose calculations for fluoroscopic and interventional procedures.

Computed Tomography (CT)

Clinical training in CT includes image quality and dose assessment, CTDI measurements, evaluation of CT protocols and dose metrics, and understanding protocol optimization for different clinical applications.

Experience includes assessment of CTDIvol, DLP, SSDE, image quality, and the relationship between acquisition parameters, reconstruction techniques, patient size, and radiation dose.

Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI)

Clinical training in MRI includes system performance evaluation, image quality assessment, and quality control using ACR and site-specific phantoms.

Experience includes assessment of geometric accuracy, spatial resolution, slice thickness and position, image uniformity, ghosting, low-contrast detectability, and RF coil performance, along with training in MRI safety and imaging principles.

Mammography & Digital Breast Tomosynthesis

Clinical training includes quality control and performance evaluation of digital mammography and digital breast tomosynthesis systems, with emphasis on image quality, radiation dose, system performance, and regulatory compliance.

Experience includes phantom image-quality assessment, artifact evaluation, detector performance, and review of technologist quality-control records.

Ultrasound

Clinical training in diagnostic ultrasound includes system performance evaluation and quality control, with assessment of image quality and transducer performance.

Experience includes evaluation of uniformity, depth of penetration, distance measurement accuracy, axial and lateral resolution, and identification of image artifacts.

Nuclear Medicine

Clinical training includes quality control and performance evaluation of nuclear medicine imaging systems, including gamma cameras, SPECT, SPECT/CT, PET, PET/CT, well counters, and dose calibrators.

Experience includes assessment of system uniformity, spatial resolution, sensitivity, energy resolution, and image quality, along with exposure to dose calibrator and well-counter quality control and clinical theranostic procedures.

Radiation Safety & Clinical Support

Clinical experience includes radiation safety assessments, shielding design calculations, patient dose evaluation, and application of institutional and regulatory requirements in diagnostic imaging.

Additional experience includes review of imaging quality-control programs, investigation of equipment performance issues, preparation of technical reports, and collaboration with medical physicists, radiologists, technologists, engineers, and vendors.
